Editorial Policy

How Clinovian's published analysis is written, checked, and corrected.

This policy governs the Insights library, the fictional specimen documents, and every factual or regulatory claim published on this site. It exists so a reader can evaluate the reliability of the material before relying on it.

Standards

Authorship and review

Published analysis is produced by Clinovian's physician-led practice and reviewed before publication for clinical reasoning, source accuracy, and scope discipline. Where a piece is authored or reviewed by a contributor other than the practice, the piece says so. Articles carry a publication date and, where materially revised, a visible update date.

Sourcing

Regulatory, statistical, and policy claims are tied to primary sources — CMS and Federal Register publications, HHS-OIG reports, peer-reviewed or society guidance, and named public surveys — and are dated. Population-level statistics are presented as population-level findings, never as Clinovian outcomes. Time-sensitive claims (fees, effective dates, published federal data) are tracked in an internal claims register with named review triggers and are re-checked at least quarterly and on every relevant CMS, DOL, or Treasury notice.

What we do not publish

No fabricated or anonymous testimonials. No generalized overturn or recovery rates. No outcome guarantees. No case studies without a stated denominator, source, period, exclusions, and client permission. No legal advice, coding determinations, or payer coverage conclusions — those remain with appropriately licensed professionals.

Specimens

Sample deliverables are fabricated scenarios with realistic clinical detail. They are labeled as fictional, carry assumptions and limitations, and demonstrate format and reasoning method — not real cases, real payers' determinations, or promised results.

Corrections

If you believe something published here is inaccurate or out of date, email contact@clinovian.com with the page, the claim, and the source you are relying on. Substantiated corrections are made on the page itself; materially significant corrections are noted with a dated correction line. Superseded documents are marked as superseded rather than silently removed.
