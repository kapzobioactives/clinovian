Send one de-identified matter. We'll confirm fit, scope, and fee.

No PHI and no commitment. The initial review confirms service fit, whether physician input may be useful, the documents required, and the appropriate paid engagement. It does not include a substantive clinical argument, pursuit recommendation, or deadline advice.

What to send
For denial matters
  • Denial rationale, payer and plan type
  • Service line, approximate value, and current appeal level
  • Denial date or client-supplied appeal deadline, if known
  • Brief description of why the case is stuck
For Federal IDR matters
  • Current IDR stage and client-supplied submission deadline
  • Service category, dispute configuration, and approximate line-item count
  • Central clinical distinction and documents currently available
  • Existing narrative status and preferred partnership type

Do not send PHI. De-identify all materials before sending. The initial fit assessment does not require protected health information. PHI workflow is formalized only after scope approval and BAA execution.
