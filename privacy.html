Privacy Policy

Version 2.0 · last updated 14 September 2026 · supersedes the June 2026 notice

This notice describes what the public website collects and what happens to it. Client work under a signed engagement is governed by that engagement and its Business Associate Agreement, which take precedence over this page.

1. What we collect

Business contact details you type in. The inquiry form asks for your name, email address, organisation, role and which service or workflow you are asking about. These reach us by email through our form provider.

What you write in the free-text fields. If you use the case-fit path, you may add a short de-identified description of a dispute, the payer’s stated rationale, an appeal stage, a deadline you tell us about, and a disputed value. We ask you not to include patient identifiers, and the form’s pattern check is a prompt, not a control. If you send something you should not have, email us and we will delete it.

Where your inquiry came from. Three hidden fields travel with the form: which service your link was for, which of our own pages you came from, and a campaign tag if one was in the URL. This is how we tell which routes bring real inquiries. No cookie is set and no third party is contacted for this, and nothing at all is recorded for someone who never submits the form. If you arrive from another website, that referrer is discarded rather than stored — a search query or a private URL elsewhere never reaches us.

Scheduling data, if you book a call. Calls are booked through Calendly, which collects your name, email, chosen time and anything you type into its booking fields. That happens on Calendly’s systems under their privacy notice, not ours.

Technical request data. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Serving a page necessarily involves your IP address and browser user-agent reaching the host and our form and font providers. We do not control those logs and do not receive them as a usable dataset.

What we do not collect. No analytics or tracking product is installed on this site, and no cookie is set by us. The three attribution fields above are not a tracker: they are part of a form you chose to send, and they see nothing if you do not send it. We have not built an audience profile, a session recording, or a marketing pixel. If that changes, this notice changes first and a consent mechanism will be added before any such tool is enabled — not after.

3. How we use it

To reply to you, to judge whether the work is a fit, and to agree scope and fee. That is the whole purpose. We do not sell, rent or share inquiry data, and we do not send marketing to an address that reached us through this form unless you ask us to.

Under a signed engagement we may keep de-identified, non-client-identifiable learning points for quality improvement and methodology work. Those exclude PHI, client identity, payer contract terms, case identifiers and financial identifiers, and are governed by the engagement rather than by this page.

5. Your rights over what you sent us

Email contact@clinovian.com and ask us to give you a copy of what you sent, correct it, or delete it. We will act on a deletion request for inquiry data without asking you to justify it. Requests are answered within 30 days.

Two honest limits. We can delete our copy and ask our providers to delete theirs; we cannot guarantee removal from a provider’s backups within their own cycle. And where the data is part of a live engagement, professional and contractual record-keeping obligations may require us to retain some of it — we will say so plainly rather than quietly keeping it.

Depending on where you live, you may have statutory rights beyond these. We do not require you to invoke a statute to get a copy or a deletion.

6. Where the work happens

Clinical review is performed from India. This is disclosed before any engagement, not discovered afterwards. Cross-border handling of client records is governed by the Business Associate Agreement and engagement terms, and by whatever your organisation approves — see Security & PHI Handling for what is and is not established.

7. Changes

This notice is versioned. The version number and date at the top change whenever the substance changes, and a substantive change will not be made retroactively to cover collection that already happened.
