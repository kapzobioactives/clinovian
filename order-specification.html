Order Specification

Version 2026.09 · effective 13 September 2026

This page governs. Every price, unit, turnaround and pilot statement elsewhere on this site is a summary of what is defined here. Where a service page, the FAQ, the machine-readable service catalog or the intake form differs from this page, this page governs until a signed order says otherwise.

1. What one unit of work is

A standard unit is one physician review of one denial or one requested service, for one patient encounter, under one plan policy, producing one written deliverable. It includes:

  • up to 150 pages of agreed record;
  • up to three distinct clinical issues within that denial;
  • one applicable plan policy or criteria set, supplied by the client or otherwise properly authorised;
  • one written deliverable in the format published on the relevant service page;
  • one revision within 10 business days of delivery (see section 4).

Anything beyond those limits is a separate unit or a re-quote, agreed in writing before work continues. Clinovian will say so before starting, not after delivering.

3. Pilot and evaluation eligibility

Two evaluations are published. Both are discounts on a defined set of work, not credit toward anything in the catalog.

Three-case denial evaluation — $1,000

Covers three standard units. Compared with three standard memos at $450, that is a 25.9% discount. Cases outside the standard unit — observation, post-acute, DRG validation or IDR — consume more than one unit each and are not included at this price. Where a submitted case converts to more than one unit, Clinovian will say so before accepting it, and the client chooses whether to substitute a different case or pay the difference at the published rate.

Three-dossier federal IDR evaluation — $1,800

Covers three single-dispute IDR dossiers. Compared with three at the $750 starting price, that is a 20% discount. A proposed batch, a mixed inventory, or a dispute requiring more than one clinical comparator is quoted separately.

Both evaluations: cases must be accepted by Clinovian on suitability before the fee is charged. A case Clinovian declines is replaced or refunded pro rata at the client's choice. Evaluation pricing applies once per organisation.

4. Revisions and new records

  • Included: one revision per unit, requested within 10 business days of delivery, addressing clarity, emphasis, structure or a factual correction within the record already supplied. Returned within 2 business days.
  • Not included: a revision that follows newly supplied records, a changed plan policy, a different denial rationale, a new appeal level, or a change of requested service. That is new work and is quoted as a new unit.
  • Clinovian will not revise a conclusion to make it stronger than the evidence supports. Where a client asks for that, Clinovian will explain what the record does and does not establish and leave the conclusion as it stands.

5. The delivery clock

Published turnarounds measure production time only. They start when Clinovian confirms in writing that inputs are complete and the fee is agreed — not when you first make contact.

Business days are Monday to Friday excluding Indian public holidays, and the reference time zone is IST (UTC+5:30). A request confirmed after 18:00 IST starts the clock on the next business day.

Plan for the whole sequence, not the production number. A 1-business-day response followed by onboarding and a 48–72-hour production window does not fit a peer-to-peer call in the morning. If you have a deadline, say so in the first message.

6. Urgent and concurrent work

Same-day and concurrent-review turnarounds are available only to clients who are already onboarded, with an agreed record-transfer route and reserved capacity confirmed in writing for the period concerned. Clinovian does not accept a first engagement on an urgent timeline.

If reserved capacity is not available, Clinovian will say so immediately rather than accept and hope. Clinovian does not calculate, verify or assume responsibility for any appeal, filing or peer-to-peer deadline; it works to the deadline the client supplies.

7. Recurring capacity

  • Minimum term: 3 months. Cancellation: 30 days' written notice, effective at the end of the notice period.
  • Unused units roll over for one month only and then lapse. They are not refundable.
  • Overage is billed at the published single-order rate for the service, and requires written approval before the work starts.
  • Clinovian may suspend intake where committed volume is consistently exceeded, rather than deliver late or below standard. Notice is given before suspension takes effect.
  • A monthly commitment does not reserve same-day capacity unless section 6 is also satisfied.

8. When Clinovian declines

Clinovian declines cases that fall outside its competence or scope — including any matter requiring a U.S. licensed determination, a specialty-board opinion, a treating-clinician role, or legal representation. See case suitability.

A case declined before work starts is not charged. A case declined after review has begun, because the record turns out not to support the analysis requested, is charged at half the agreed fee and the client receives the written explanation of why — which is often the useful output.

9. What is never included at any price

  • Final code assignment, code selection, or grouper determination.
  • Filing, submission, portal access or representation before any payer, reviewer or IDR entity.
  • Legal advice, parity determinations, or jurisdiction-specific opinion.
  • A treating-clinician opinion, or any determination requiring U.S. licensure or board certification.
  • Any prediction, forecast or guarantee of recovery, payment, or appeal outcome.
  • Deadline calculation or verification.

10. Payment

Fixed fee, agreed in writing before work starts. No hourly billing. Invoiced on delivery for single orders and monthly in advance for recurring capacity, payable within 15 days, in USD unless otherwise agreed. Applicable taxes are additional. Deliverable ownership, confidentiality, permitted white-labelling, liability allocation and dispute arrangements are in the terms of service and in any signed order between the parties.

Questions about scope or units before you order: ask for a fit assessment.
