Terms of Service

Last updated: June 2026

Nature of Services

Clinovian provides physician-authored clinical consulting services for medical-necessity disputes. Clinovian is not a law firm, not a medical practice, and does not provide legal advice, medical treatment, or binding clinical determinations. Legal strategy, filing decisions, parity enforcement, and jurisdiction-specific advice remain with the client's counsel or designated legal representative.

Clinical Consulting Scope

Clinical escalation methodology is led by an MBBS physician with payer-side utilization-management review experience. The deliverable is clinical reasoning and criteria-mapped analysis for use by the client's own team — not a licensed clinical opinion, a treating-physician determination, or formal U.S.-licensed clinical sign-off. Where formal U.S.-licensed sign-off is independently required for a matter, that responsibility remains with the client's own licensed clinical staff or designated reviewer. The scope of each engagement is defined in the engagement documentation.

De-identified Learning Rights

Engagements are structured so Clinovian may retain de-identified, non-client-identifiable learning points for internal quality improvement, pattern analysis, and methodology development. No PHI, client-confidential information, payer contract terms, or case-identifying details are reused.

Limitation of Liability

Clinovian's liability for any engagement is limited to the fees paid for that specific engagement. Clinovian does not guarantee appeal outcomes, arbitration results, or payer decisions.
