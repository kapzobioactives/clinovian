Terms of Service

Version 2.0 · last updated 14 September 2026 · supersedes the June 2026 terms

These are the public terms for buying work from Clinovian. Operational detail — what one unit of work is, what a revision covers, when the clock starts — lives in the Order Specification, which forms part of these terms. Where a signed engagement letter says something different, the signed document governs.

1. Nature of services

Clinovian provides physician-authored clinical consulting for medical-necessity disputes. Clinovian is not a law firm, not a medical practice, and does not provide legal advice, medical treatment, or binding clinical determinations. Legal strategy, filing decisions, parity enforcement and jurisdiction-specific advice remain with your counsel or designated legal representative.

The methodology is led by an MBBS physician with payer-side utilization-management review experience. The deliverable is clinical reasoning and criteria-mapped analysis for your own team to use — not a licensed clinical opinion, a treating-physician determination, or formal U.S.-licensed sign-off. Where formal U.S.-licensed sign-off is independently required, that responsibility stays with your licensed clinical staff.

Clinovian does not assign final codes, impersonate a treating clinician, file on your behalf, or act as your legal representative.

2. Fees, invoicing and tax

Fees are fixed per deliverable and agreed in writing before substantive work begins. There is no hourly billing and no running meter. Published starting prices are entry points, not quotes; the quoted fee for your matter is what applies.

Invoices are issued in United States dollars and are payable within 14 days of the invoice date unless the engagement says otherwise. Prices are exclusive of any tax, levy or withholding. Where a withholding applies to a cross-border payment, the invoiced amount is the amount Clinovian is to receive net of it, unless agreed otherwise in writing before the order.

Bank charges on international transfers are borne by the paying party.

3. When work starts, and what starts it

Work begins when scope, fee and turnaround are confirmed in writing and the agreed inputs are complete. The delivery clock is defined in the Order Specification. A fit assessment is not an engagement and creates no obligation on either side.

4. Revisions, new records and scope change

Each deliverable includes the revision allowance stated in the Order Specification. A revision means correcting, clarifying or tightening the delivered analysis on the record already supplied.

Records that arrive after delivery, a change of payer, a change of disputed service, or a new denial rationale are a new scope, not a revision, and are quoted separately. Clinovian will say which of the two something is before doing the work, not after invoicing it.

5. Cancellation, refunds and declined cases

  • Before work starts: cancel for any reason, no charge.
  • After work starts, before delivery: a pro-rata fee reflecting work actually performed, and you receive whatever has been produced.
  • After delivery: the fee is payable. Clinovian does not refund on the basis that a payer or arbitrator decided against you, because the outcome is not what was sold.
  • Cases Clinovian declines: no fee. Within an evaluation package, a declined case does not consume a slot — substitute another or take a pro-rata credit.
  • Missed delivery window: if Clinovian misses an agreed window for reasons within its control, you may cancel and pay nothing, or accept a revised window and a reduced fee.

6. What you receive and what you may do with it

On payment, the deliverable is yours. You may edit it, incorporate it into your own filings, and submit it under your own name. Partners may white-label it, subject to the credential-transparency condition below.

Clinovian retains its methods, templates and formats, which are not transferred by delivery. White-labelling may not be used to misrepresent who performed the review or their credentials — if an end client or an adjudicator asks, the answer must be accurate.

7. Confidentiality and conflicts

Each side keeps the other’s confidential information confidential, including payer contract terms, pricing and case material, and uses it only for the engagement. The de-identified learning rights described in the Privacy Policy are the one carve-out, and they exclude PHI, client identity, contract terms and case identifiers.

Clinovian performs a conflict check before accepting a matter and will decline where it has acted, or is acting, for an opposing party on the same dispute. Clinovian works for multiple clients in the same market; that alone is not a conflict, and Clinovian does not offer market exclusivity.

8. Liability

Clinovian gives no warranty as to outcome. Appeal, arbitration and payer decisions are made by third parties on records and arguments Clinovian does not control, and no result is promised anywhere on this site or in any deliverable.

Subject to the paragraph below, each party’s aggregate liability arising out of an engagement is limited to the fees paid for that engagement, and neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including lost recoveries and lost profits.

That cap is a contractual allocation, not a statement of law. Liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited in the applicable jurisdiction — including fraud, wilful misconduct, and anything a mandatory rule preserves — is not excluded or limited by these terms. Whether the cap is enforceable in your jurisdiction is a question for your counsel, and Clinovian does not assert that it is enforceable everywhere.

9. Governing law and disputes

These public terms do not fix a forum. Governing law and dispute resolution are set in the signed engagement for the specific matter, so that they can be agreed with your procurement and legal teams rather than imposed by a web page. Before any formal step, both sides will attempt to resolve the matter in good faith by direct discussion.

10. Changes

These terms are versioned. A change applies to orders placed after the change; it does not alter an engagement already agreed.
