## Public-language guardrails

- Use “certified IDR entity” and “payment determination,” not “arbitrator” as the default public wording.
- Keep QPA, initial payment, initiating-party offer and non-initiating-party offer separate.
- Do not describe market-rate benchmarking as a statutory factor.
- Do not promise IDR eligibility, filing, coding validation, offer selection, outcomes or turnaround by the certified IDR entity.
- Clinovian never requests portal credentials and never pays or administers federal process fees.
